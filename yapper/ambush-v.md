## Ambush V

These dialogue lines trigger when the Rival is going to ambush V, generally 1 or 2 minutes out from arriving at where V currently is in the game world as they recieve the message. V can (and often will) be in a different location when the "ambush" actually happens. If the rival can't find V, they will get out of their car and look for a few minutes then leave unless V attacks them or they find V. that triggers the "evade" dialogue.

Rivals can ambush V if V's heat level is high (V's been killing a lot of the gang), or the rivals heat level is high (V's been pissing off the rival specifically)
